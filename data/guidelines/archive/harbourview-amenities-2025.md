# Harbourview Tower — Amenities

Updated March 2025.

## Pool and hot tub

Open daily 6:00 am to 11:00 pm. Maximum two guests per unit.

## Party room

Available until 11:00 pm. Book at least 3 days in advance at the front desk. A $50 refundable deposit is required. Maximum 25 people.

## Gym

Open 5:00 am to midnight. Fob access.
