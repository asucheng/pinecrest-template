# Pinecrest Property Management — Community Guidelines

Effective January 1, 2025. These guidelines apply to all Pinecrest buildings.

## 1. Quiet hours

1.1 Quiet hours are 11:00 pm to 8:00 am.
1.2 During quiet hours, keep noise at a level that cannot be heard in neighbouring units.

## 2. Garbage and recycling

2.1 Garbage chutes may be used from 7:00 am to 11:00 pm.
2.2 Bulky items must be booked for pickup with the front desk.

## 3. Pets

3.1 A maximum of three pets is permitted per unit.
3.2 Dogs must be leashed in common areas.
3.3 Owners must clean up after their pets.

## 4. Moving in and out

4.1 The service elevator must be booked at least 48 hours in advance.
4.2 Moves are permitted Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm.
4.3 A refundable deposit of $200 is required for each elevator booking.

## 5. Balconies

5.1 Electric and propane grills are permitted. Charcoal barbecues are not allowed.

## 6. Smoking

6.1 Smoking is prohibited in all common areas.

## 7. Fines

7.1 The fine schedule is:

| Violation | Fine |
| --- | --- |
| Noise, first notice | $50 |
| Noise, second notice | $150 |
| Noise, third and later notices | $300 |
| Pet waste not cleaned up | $100 |
| Move without an elevator booking | $200 |
| Smoking in a common area | $150 |
| Items left in a hallway | $50 |
| Short-term rental | $500 |

## 8. Disputing a fine

8.1 A resident may dispute a fine by writing to the property manager within 30 days of the fine notice.
