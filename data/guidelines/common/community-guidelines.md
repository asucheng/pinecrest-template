# Pinecrest Property Management — Community Guidelines

Effective January 1, 2026. These guidelines apply to all Pinecrest buildings. Where a building's own rules are stricter, the building rules apply.

## 1. Quiet hours

1.1 Quiet hours are 11:00 pm to 7:00 am, unless your building's rules set different hours.
1.2 During quiet hours, keep music, TV and conversation at a level that cannot be heard in neighbouring units or hallways.
1.3 Renovation and construction noise is permitted Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm only.

## 2. Garbage and recycling

2.1 Garbage chutes may be used from 7:00 am to 11:00 pm.
2.2 Bulky items (furniture, mattresses, appliances) must be booked for pickup with the front desk at least 48 hours in advance.
2.3 Nothing may be left in hallways, stairwells or chute rooms, including bags waiting to be taken down.

## 3. Pets

3.1 A maximum of two pets is permitted per unit.
3.2 Dogs must be leashed in all common areas, including elevators and lobbies.
3.3 Owners must clean up after their pets immediately, inside and outside the building.
3.4 All pets must be registered with management within 30 days of moving in or of the pet's arrival.

## 4. Moving in and out

4.1 The service elevator must be booked with the front desk at least 72 hours in advance.
4.2 Moves are permitted Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm. No moves on Sundays or statutory holidays.
4.3 A refundable deposit of $250 is required for each elevator booking.

## 5. Balconies

5.1 Only electric grills are permitted. Charcoal and propane barbecues are not allowed.
5.2 Laundry, towels and rugs may not be hung over balcony railings.
5.3 Do not feed birds from balconies.

## 6. Smoking and vaping

6.1 Smoking and vaping of any substance is prohibited in all common areas and within 9 metres of building entrances.

## 7. Leasing

7.1 Short-term rentals (for example through Airbnb) are prohibited. The minimum lease term is six months.

## 8. Fines

8.1 Management may issue fines for violations of these guidelines or of building rules. The current fine schedule is:

| Violation | Fine |
| --- | --- |
| Noise, first notice | $100 |
| Noise, second notice | $250 |
| Noise, third and later notices | $500 |
| Pet waste not cleaned up | $150 |
| Move without an elevator booking | $300 |
| Smoking or vaping in a common area | $250 |
| Items left in a hallway | $75 |
| Short-term rental | $1,000 |

8.2 Fines are added to the resident's account and are due within 30 days.

## 9. Disputing a fine

9.1 A resident may dispute a fine by writing to the property manager within 14 days of the fine notice.
9.2 Disputes are reviewed by the property manager, whose decision is final.

## 10. Contact

10.1 Each building's front desk is staffed 7:00 am to 11:00 pm.
10.2 For emergencies outside front desk hours, call the 24-hour Pinecrest line at 416-555-0188.
