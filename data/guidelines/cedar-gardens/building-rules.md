# Cedar Gardens — Building Rules

These rules apply to residents of Cedar Gardens in addition to the Pinecrest Community Guidelines.

## Quiet hours

Cedar Gardens follows the community-wide quiet hours of 11:00 pm to 7:00 am.

## Parking

Cedar Gardens has no visitor parking. Guests must use street parking and follow City of Toronto parking rules.

## Community garden

Garden plots are assigned each March. Applications open March 1 at the management office. Each plot costs $25 per season.
