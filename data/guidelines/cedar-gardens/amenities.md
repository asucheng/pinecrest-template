# Cedar Gardens — Amenities

Updated January 2026.

## Outdoor pool

The outdoor pool is open seasonally, from Victoria Day weekend to Labour Day, 7:00 am to 10:00 pm.

## Courtyard

Cedar Gardens does not have a party room. The courtyard may be reserved for daytime gatherings of up to 20 people, between 10:00 am and 8:00 pm, at no charge. Book with the management office.

## Gym

Open 6:00 am to 10:00 pm. Access with your resident fob.
