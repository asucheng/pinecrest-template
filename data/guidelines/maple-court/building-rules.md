# Maple Court — Building Rules

These rules apply to residents of Maple Court in addition to the Pinecrest Community Guidelines.

## Quiet hours

Maple Court follows the community-wide quiet hours of 11:00 pm to 7:00 am.

## Guest parking

Guests may park in the visitor lot for up to 48 hours with a parking permit from the concierge. Permits are issued in person at the concierge desk.

## Bicycles

Bicycles may be kept in units or in the B1 bike storage room.

## Recycling

The recycling room is on level B1 and is open 7:00 am to 11:00 pm.
