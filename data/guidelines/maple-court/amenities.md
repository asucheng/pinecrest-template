# Maple Court — Amenities

Updated January 2026.

## Party room (ground floor)

The party room may be used until 11:00 pm. Book at least 14 days in advance through the concierge. A $150 refundable deposit is required. Maximum 40 people.

## Gym (level B1)

Open 5:00 am to 11:00 pm. Access with your resident fob.

## Guest suite

A furnished guest suite is available for residents' visitors at $90 per night, for a maximum of 3 nights per stay. Book through the concierge.

Maple Court does not have a pool.
