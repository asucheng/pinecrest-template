# Harbourview Tower — Amenities

Updated January 2026.

## Pool and hot tub (level 3)

Open daily 6:00 am to 9:00 pm. Closed the first Monday of every month for maintenance. Maximum two guests per unit. Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

## Party room (level 2)

The party room may be used until 10:00 pm, in line with Harbourview quiet hours. Book at least 7 days in advance at the front desk. A $100 refundable deposit is required. Maximum 30 people. The room must be cleaned and vacated by 10:30 pm.

## Gym (level 3)

Open 24 hours. Access with your resident fob. Guests are not permitted in the gym.

## Rooftop terrace

Open 8:00 am to 10:00 pm, first come, first served. No glass containers.
