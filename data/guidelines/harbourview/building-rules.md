# Harbourview Tower — Building Rules

These rules apply to residents of Harbourview Tower in addition to the Pinecrest Community Guidelines.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours at Harbourview Tower are 10:00 pm to 7:00 am. This is stricter than the community-wide hours because of the building's open atrium, which carries sound between floors.

## Guest parking

Guest parking is on level P1. Guests may park for up to 24 hours at a time. Every guest vehicle must be registered in advance through the ResidentHub portal; unregistered vehicles are towed at the owner's expense.

## Bicycles

Bicycles must be stored in the P1 bike room. Bicycles are not permitted in units, hallways or elevators, except to bring them to and from the bike room using the service elevator.

## Parcels

Parcels are held in the parcel room off the lobby. Residents receive a ResidentHub notification and must collect parcels within 5 days.

## Balconies

Nothing may be attached to balcony glass, including decals, signs and screens.
